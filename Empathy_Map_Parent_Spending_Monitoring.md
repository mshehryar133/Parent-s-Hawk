# Empathy Map — Parent Spending Monitoring System

## How Might We Statement

> **How might we help parents maintain a complete and tamper-resistant record of their children's card and online spending?**

---

## Empathy Map

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| **Says** | "I want to know where my child is spending money."  <br>"Transaction notifications can be deleted."  <br>"I need a reliable record of every transaction."  <br>"I don't want my child to hide their spending from me." |
| **Thinks** | "What if my child deletes the transaction notification?"  <br>"Is there a way to see the complete transaction history?"  <br>"Can I trust the information I'm seeing?"  <br>"I need something that my child cannot easily manipulate." |
| **Does** | Checks transaction notifications on their phone.  <br>Reviews bank/card transaction history.  <br>Asks their child about unfamiliar purchases.  <br>Checks spending patterns when concerned. |
| **Feels** | Worried when transactions disappear.  <br>Frustrated when notifications are deleted.  <br>Concerned about unnecessary or unauthorized spending.  <br>Relieved when they have a reliable record of transactions. |
| **Pains** | Transaction notifications can be deleted.  <br>Important spending information may be difficult to track.  <br>Children may hide or deny certain purchases.  <br>Parents may have to manually check transactions.  <br>Lack of a centralized spending record. |
| **Gains** | A complete transaction history.  <br>Tamper-resistant records.  <br>Automatic spending reports.  <br>Alerts for unusual or excessive spending.  <br>Better understanding of children's spending habits.  <br>Greater financial awareness and control. |

---

## Key Insight

The main problem is **not simply deleted notifications**. The deeper problem is that parents may not have a **separate, reliable, tamper-resistant source of transaction information**.

Therefore, the system should maintain transaction records independently from ordinary phone notifications.
